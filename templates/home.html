The Three Pillars of Ayurvedic Balance

In classical Ayurveda, your unique mind-body constitution (Prakriti) is composed of three vital energies. Health is the dynamic harmony of these forces.

Air & Space
💨

Vata Dosha

Governs all biological movement, nerve impulses, respiration, and cellular circulation. When aggravated, it causes joint aches, anxiety, insomnia, and dryness.

  • Balanced with warm sesame oil
  • Nourishing, grounding foods
  • Gentle restorative routines
Fire & Water
🔥

Pitta Dosha

Governs digestion (Agni), metabolism, body temperature, and intelligence. Imbalance manifests as hyperacidity, skin rashes, inflammation, and irritability.

  • Cooled with Ghritham & Amalaki
  • Sweet, bitter & cooling diet
  • Stress-release pranayama
Earth & Water
🌊

Kapha Dosha

Provides biological lubrication, structural stability, physical strength, and immunity (Ojas). Excess leads to lethargy, sinus congestion, weight gain, and sluggishness.

  • Stimulated with Trikatu & herbs
  • Light, warm & pungent diet
  • Daily active movement & detox
